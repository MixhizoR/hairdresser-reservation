# Product Guidelines: HairMan Studio

## Tone of Voice
- **Professional & Efficient**: Use clear, direct language that emphasizes ease of use and professionalism.
- **Supportive**: Provide helpful feedback and instructions, especially during the booking process.
- **Trustworthy**: Use a tone that conveys reliability and security.

## User Experience (UX) Principles
1. **Mobile-First Design**: Ensure the reservation flow is optimized for mobile devices, as many customers will book on the go.
2. **Instant Feedback**: Provide visual and audio confirmation for every user action (e.g., successful booking, real-time updates).
3. **Frictionless Navigation**: Keep the booking process short and intuitive, with a maximum of three steps to complete a reservation.
4. **Consistency**: Use a unified set of UI components and interactions across all pages and roles.

## Visual Style
- **Corporate Minimalist**: Focus on whitespace, clean typography, and a refined color palette (e.g., neutral greys, deep blacks, and a single professional accent color).
- **Glassmorphism & Depth**: Use subtle depth effects and modern transparency where it enhances the clean, sophisticated look.
- **High-Quality Icons**: Use a consistent, high-quality icon set (e.g., Lucide) to aid navigation and visual hierarchy.

## Accessibility
- **WCAG 2.1 Compliance**: Aim for AA standards, ensuring high color contrast and full keyboard navigability.
- **Intuitive Cues**: Provide multiple ways to perceive information (e.g., text labels along with icons, audio along with visual notifications).
