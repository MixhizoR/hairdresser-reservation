# Product Definition: HairMan Studio

## Overview
HairMan Studio is a professional, clean reservation management system designed for a high-end barber experience. It focuses on providing a first-class digital interface for customers while maintaining robust administrative controls for staff.

## Target Audience
- **Customers**: Individuals seeking a seamless and professional way to book hair appointments.
- **Barbers & Admins**: Professionals managing their schedules and the overall system through a centralized dashboard.

## Core Goals
1. **Seamless Booking**: Provide a frictionless experience for customers to find and reserve open slots.
2. **Real-time Updates**: Ensure all appointment changes and status updates are synchronized instantly across the platform.
3. **Premium Branding**: Maintain a high-quality, professional brand image throughout the user journey.

## Key Features
- **Socket.io Dashboards**: Real-time synchronization of appointments and status updates for instant feedback.
- **Advanced Security & Honeypot**: Integrated bot protection and robust authentication to ensure a safe environment.
- **Premium Audio Feedback**: High-quality notification tones to enhance the user experience and professionalism.

## Aesthetic Vision
- **Corporate Minimalist**: A clean, professional, and sophisticated design language that focuses on usability and modern aesthetics.
